This is an OpenClaw skill providing comprehensive knowledge of the I Ching (易经, Book of Changes), the oldest and most influential of the Chinese classical texts.

### Divination: Coin Toss Method

1. **Prepare**: Hold 3 coins (preferably old Chinese coins or any 3 identical coins)
2. **Assign values**: Heads = 3, Tails = 2
3. **Toss**: Throw all 3 coins 6 times
4. **Record**: From bottom to top, draw lines based on totals:
   - 8 or 6 = Broken line ( Yin, 〃)
   - 9 or 7 = Solid line ( Yang, —)
5. **Note changes**: 6 (old yin) and 9 (old yang) are changing lines
6. **Consult**: Use the resulting hexagram(s) for interpretation

### 核心概念

#### 八卦 (8 Trigrams)

| 符号 | 中文 | 拼音 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|------|
| ☰ | 乾 | Qian | 创造、天、刚强 |
| ☱ | 兑 | Dui | 喜悦、泽、愉悦 |
| ☲ | 离 | Li | 附着、火、光明 |
| ☳ | 震 | Zhen | 震动、雷、行动 |
| ☴ | 巽 | Xun | 深入、风、柔顺 |
| ☵ | 坎 | Kan | 坎陷、水、危险 |
| ☶ | 艮 | Gen | 静止、山、稳固 |
| ☷ | 坤 | Kun | 接受、地、柔顺 |

#### 爻 (Lines)

- **阳爻 (Solid line)**: — 代表积极、主动、刚强
- **阴爻 (Broken line)**: 〃 代表消极、被动、柔顺
- **变爻**: 老阴(6)变阳，老阳(9)变阴

### 占卜方法：铜钱法

1. **准备**: 准备3枚铜钱（或任何相同的硬币）
2. **赋值**: 正面=3，反面=2
3. **掷钱**: 掷3枚铜钱共6次
4. **记录**: 从下往上，根据总数画线：
   - 8或6 = 阴爻（断开的线）
   - 9或7 = 阳爻（完整的线）
5. **标记变爻**: 6和9是变爻，会发生变化
6. **解读**: 根据所得卦象进行解读
